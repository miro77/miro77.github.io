The curve updates while you move a slider. The model runs in your browser as WebAssembly; nothing is sent to a server. Field strength is derived from the basic transmission loss Lb as E = 139.3 + 20 log10 f − Lb + 10 log10 P in dB(µV/m), with f in MHz and the e.r.p. P in kW.

Based on the U.S. reference implementation of Recommendation ITU-R P.528-5. Results are informative and not an official ITU prediction. Source: github.com/miro77/p528-wasm
